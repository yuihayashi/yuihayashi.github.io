RESEARCH

Research

研究

01

Bayesian Inference for X-ray and Neutron Scattering

X線・中性子散乱のベイズ推論

I apply Bayesian inference to small-angle scattering (SAS) data from X-ray and neutron experiments to select structural models of the sample and to estimate their physical parameters. The Bayesian approach quantifies the uncertainty of each model and, through the evaluation of marginal likelihoods, enables appropriate model selection while avoiding overfitting.
